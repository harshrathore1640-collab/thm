About THM by Praviin Janngir

Crafting Luxury Wood Solutions for Distinguished Interiors

Under the expert guidance of Praviin Janngir, THM has emerged as a premier destination for high-end architectural millwork and bespoke wood solutions. We specialize in transforming architectural visions into breathtaking wooden realities.

Trusted by top-tier interior designers, architects, and discerning clients, our manufacturing atelier combines time-honored woodworking heritage with state-of-the-art precision engineering to deliver flawless wall paneling, grand doors, and custom furniture.
